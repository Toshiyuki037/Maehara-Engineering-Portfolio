ABOUT

Building the future one system at a time

Outside of engineering, I shoot sports photography for Orange Media Network, capturing moments that connect me to my community. I also spend time surfing, spearfishing, and exploring the outdoors with my friends and family.

"I believe great engineering is measured not by what we can create, but by how what we create improves the world around us."

I am a sophomore Electrical & Computer Engineering student in the Honors College at Oregon State University, from Oahu, Hawaii. My current work involves the application of embedded systems, signal processing, and hardware-aware machine learning.

I currently conduct research in Jason Clark’s and Matthew Johnston’s labs, exploring neural networks on FPGA-integrated hardware and modeling calibration drift in noninvasive blood pressure devices.

This portfolio documents the research, projects, and technical work shaping my growth as an engineer committed to building systems that leave a real-world impact.
