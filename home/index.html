ABOUT

I believe technology matters most when it improves the lives of others.

I am a sophomore Electrical & Computer Engineering student in Oregon State University’s Honors College, drawn to problems where computation has to leave the screen and work in the physical world.

That curiosity has taken me from research in FPGA acceleration and biomedical sensing to building wearable vision systems, autonomous fabrication tools, and new approaches to manufacturing. Across all of it, I am trying to make complex technology more useful, capable, and human.
